Boss fight: how the dragon battle works

Every chore a parent approves is a hit on the dragon, and harder chores hit harder. A chore sent back, or 48 hours with no chore done, lets the dragon strike back. Now and then it uses a special attack on its own. If your health runs out you are knocked back to the village to rest: your health refills and the dragon gets a little back. Nobody loses their progress.
