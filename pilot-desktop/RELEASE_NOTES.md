## Pilot for Mac {{VERSION}}

Pilot is the MTRX Horizyn AI assistant: a friendly robot that sits on top of everything on your Mac. Click it to chat, get help with writing and code, or have it look at your screen when you ask. It runs on your own AI key (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, or any compatible API), so your AI provider bills you directly and MTRX never sees your key.

One file for every Mac: Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 12 or newer.

### What's new in 1.1.0

- **You can type in the chat again.** In 1.0, the chat box didn't take your typing on a real Mac: keys went to the app behind it. Now the chat comes forward when you open it, the message box is ready for typing, paste, copy, select all and undo work, and when you close the chat your keyboard goes back to the app you were using.
- **The robot comes to life.** It moves around your screen by itself, gently: it walks and runs along the bottom, flies with a soft glow trail, climbs up the screen edges, does tricks (a backflip, a spin, bounces, a peek from behind the edge) and now and then a sports trick (a football throw, a soccer goal, tennis against the screen edge). It uses the owner's 18 new pose pictures: it celebrates when something you allowed is done, does a knee slide, charges a glowing energy ball, lands like a superhero after a flight, points up when there's no key yet, and sends hearts when you say thanks. It waves when you come back to your Mac and when you open the chat, and glances at your mouse. Most of the time it just rests, breathing and blinking.
- **It never gets in the way.** Clicks go straight through to your apps except on the robot itself. It never covers the menu bar or the chat, never takes your keyboard, and keeps still while you type, while a video or game is full screen, and while the chat is open.
- **You're in charge.** Calm Mode (no wandering) and Energy (Low, Normal, Playful) are in the menu bar and in settings. "Play a Trick" in the menu bar (or right-click the robot) shows one on demand, and the Trick menu lets you pick any of them. Drag the robot anywhere: that spot becomes its home. If your Mac is set to reduce motion, Calm Mode is on unless you turn it off.
- **Screen access, your way.** Settings shows whether your Mac allows Pilot to see the screen, with Open Mac settings and Check again, and three choices: Off, Only when I ask (the default), or Help with what's on screen (a fresh screenshot with each message). Pilot never watches in the background, and the "Pilot is looking" banner shows every time.
- **Drop pictures and videos in, any size.** Drag them onto the chat or the robot, paste them, or use the paperclip. Pictures (including HEIC) are shrunk to fit your AI provider. Videos go through Google Gemini's own upload with a progress bar, or Pilot looks at evenly spaced frames from them for other providers. Files go only to your own AI provider.

### Install in 4 steps

1. Open the downloaded **.dmg** file.
2. Drag **Pilot** onto **Applications**.
3. Open **Pilot** from Applications. If your Mac says it can't check Pilot, press **Done**.
4. Open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, press **Open Anyway** next to Pilot, then **Open**.

Step 4 is only needed once, because Pilot isn't signed with an Apple developer account yet. Already have 1.0? Quit it from the menu bar, then drag the new Pilot onto Applications and choose Replace.
