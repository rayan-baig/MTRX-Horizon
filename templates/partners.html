How it works

Three steps

  1. 1

    Apply

    Tell us about your business in the Chroma Vortex partner form. It takes a couple of minutes, and we reply by email.

  2. 2

    Get a partner key

    Once we agree, you get a partner key. It opens your partner portal: the businesses you look after, any unit that's offline or low on battery, and any open alert.

  3. 3

    Your customers sign up, and you earn

    Your customer starts a free trial. We link their account to you, and you earn a share of what they pay, every month they pay.

What you earn

A share of what is really paid

  • A share of what your customers actually paid in the last 30 days. Not the price list.
  • A free trial, or an invoice that hasn't been paid, earns nothing. A cancelled invoice earns nothing; half paid earns half.
  • Your share is agreed when you join. Your statement shows each business and what you earned from it.
  • We pay it ourselves, by bank transfer, each month.

What you see

Enough to send an engineer

  • Each business you look after: its units, where they are, when each last reported, battery and signal.
  • The businesses that need a visit, at the top.
  • Never their staff, phone numbers or invoices, and never a business that isn't yours. You can't change anything in their account.

Why it matters

Why a separate alarm matters

In October 2026, hackers warmed the fridges at hundreds of supermarkets in France. Separate alarms matter: a sensor that reads the temperature on its own, apart from the fridge's controls, still tells someone when the food gets warm.

That's what CyberLogix AI's sensors do, whatever has gone wrong.
