This page is about the MTRX Horizyn website only: the home page and the pages beside it. RankUp, CyberLogix AI and OmniArmor.ai each have their own privacy page, which applies when you use that app.

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-up and no forms. The site never asks for your name, email or anything else.
  • No cookies for visitors.
  • No tracking: no analytics, no ads, no "like" buttons, and nothing loaded from any other company. Every picture, font and script comes from this site, and the site's security settings tell your browser to refuse anything else.
  • Genesis projects stay in your browser, except the text you choose to send with "Ask Genesis", which goes to our AI provider to write the answer; we keep only counts.
  • Like every website, the server notes each request it answers (see below).

What the server logs

For each request the server writes one line: the time, the network address the request came from, the page asked for and whether it worked. Behind our hosting provider, that address is usually the provider's own rather than yours. Our hosting provider keeps its own records of the requests it passes on, which can include your IP address, for as long as its own policy says. We use these lines only to keep the site working and safe, and never to work out who anyone is.

Failed sign-ins

Parts of the site are private. If someone tries a code or a sign-in there and it fails, the address it came from (for an IPv6 address, the block it belongs to) and the time are kept for 24 hours, so nobody can try codes without limit. Then they are deleted.

Cookies

Visitors get none. The site sets cookies only on the owner's own approved devices, to keep the private console locked. They are needed for that and for nothing else, are never readable by the page's own scripts, never go to any other site, and are not used to track anyone:

  • mh_d remembers that a device is approved, for up to {{ cookies.device_days }} days.
  • mh_k lasts {{ cookies.door_minutes }} minutes, for one step of signing in.
  • mh_s keeps the owner signed in, for up to {{ cookies.session_hours }} hours.

What stays in your browser

If you turn on calm mode in Settings, your browser remembers that choice on your device. It is never sent to us. The search box searches this page inside your browser; what you type is not sent anywhere.

Genesis projects stay in your browser, except the text you choose to send with "Ask Genesis", which goes to our AI provider to write the answer; we keep only counts. Your projects are saved in your browser's storage for this site, on your device only, until you delete them (each one, or Delete all) or clear this site's data. Download saves them as a file on your device, and Import reads that file back in your browser; neither sends anything to us. We can't see, recover or delete your projects for you.

Ask Genesis

Ask Genesis is optional AI help with your idea. Only when you press one of its buttons, your project's name, one-line pitch, who it's for and stage, and any question you typed, go through our server to our AI provider, Anthropic, which writes the answer. Your notes and checklist are never sent. We don't store or log that text, and the answer comes straight back to your browser. Our AI provider handles it under its own terms and privacy policy for businesses that use its service. Please don't type personal details into Genesis.

To share the free help out fairly, we count how many questions each visitor asks: the address the question came from (for an IPv6 address, the block it belongs to) and the time are kept for 24 hours, then deleted. We also keep how much each answer cost, as a number with no words in it. Nothing else about you is kept, and no cookie is set.

The numbers on the home page

The user counts and uptime on the home page are totals that the apps report to this site. They contain no one's name, email or other details.
