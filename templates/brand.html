The name

  • Write it MTRX Horizyn: MTRX in capitals, then Horizyn with a y.
  • In the logo it is set as MTRX HORIZYN.
  • Never with an o ("Horizon"), never "Matrix", and never "Mtrx".
  • The apps are RankUp, CyberLogix AI and OmniArmor.ai. MTRX Horizyn is the parent company of all three.
