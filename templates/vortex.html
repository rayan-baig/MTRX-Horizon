Before you send

Who owns my idea?

You do. Your idea stays yours: we never take a share of it without a written agreement that you (and a parent or guardian, if you're under 18) sign. Sending it doesn't create a partnership, an agreement or any obligation for either of us.

What happens after I send?

A real person reads it and emails you back, even if the answer is no. If we'd like to hear more, we'll ask in that email. We keep your message only to reply, for up to {{ keep_days }} days, then delete it.

Will you build it without me?

No. We won't publish or pass on what you send. We may already be working on something similar; if we are, we'll tell you when we reply, so you know where you stand.
